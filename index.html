Pull in, switch off, and let the granite do the rest.

Hill View Mountains is a private nature refuge in Queensland's Granite Belt. Giant boulders sit stacked across green hills, and the land climbs to a 976 metre peak with views out over the ranges.

We keep it to ten sites so every camp feels like your own. Light the fire, listen for the wallabies, and watch the stars come out over the rocks.

Hillview Nature Refuge

Protected land, by agreement

Hillview Nature Refuge was declared on 29 August 2008 under Queensland's Nature Conservation Act 1992. A nature refuge is private land that the owner has agreed with the Queensland Government to protect for its natural values, for good.

That means the bush, the rocks and the wildlife come first. Camping here is low impact by design: ten sites, no clearing, and fallen timber left where it lies as habitat.

Know before you go

Off-grid, and proud of it

This is real bush camping. Here's what's here, what isn't, and what to bring.

Water

Drinking water is available. Arrive with your tanks topped up anyway.

Bathroom

No toilets or showers. Your van must have its own toilet and grey water tank.

Power

No power. Bring solar panels or a battery setup. Generators only with permission, and never at night.

Fires

Every site has a fire pit. Use our firewood packs and always check for fire bans before you light up.

No pets

To protect the wildlife on the refuge, dogs and other pets can't come.

Cold nights

At this height the nights get cold, with frosts in winter. Pack warm layers year round.

When to come

Four real seasons

The Granite Belt is one of the few places in Queensland with proper seasons.

Dec to Feb

Summer

Warm days, cool nights

Long evenings outside and a cooler escape from the coast. Watch for fire bans.

Mar to May

Autumn

Mild, still, clear

Grape harvest time at the wineries and some of the best camping weather of the year.

Jun to Aug

Winter

Crisp days, frosty mornings

Fire season in the best sense. Big campfires, red wine and clear star-filled skies.

Sep to Nov

Spring

Fresh and green

Wildflowers in the bush, active wildlife and green hills between the boulders.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Something else? Send us a message with your booking request.

    Can I bring a tent?

    Right now our sites are for self-contained caravans and motorhomes only. Cabins are planned for later.

    Is there phone reception?

    Reception varies around the property. Some sites have a signal and some don't, which most guests see as a bonus.

    Can I collect my own firewood?

    Please don't. The property is a nature refuge, so fallen timber stays as habitat. Buy a firewood pack instead, and always follow fire bans.

    Are dogs allowed?

    No. To protect the wildlife, no dogs or other pets are allowed.

    Can big rigs get in?

    Tell us your van length when you book and we'll let you know about access and the best site for you.

    What's your cancellation policy?

    Full refund if you cancel 7 or more days before arrival. Inside 7 days, we'll refund if we can rebook your site.

    Do you sell gift vouchers?

    Yes. Send us a message with the amount and we'll organise one.
