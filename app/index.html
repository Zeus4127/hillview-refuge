Android preview

Get the Hill View app

The app has an offline map of the Stanthorpe area with road directions, the walks, the species guide and the 3D map, and it works with no signal. It's a private preview, so it isn't in the Play Store yet.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone.

Install it in 4 steps

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download the app. If the browser asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification or the Downloads app).
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. If Google Play Protect says the app is unrecognised, tap More details, then Install anyway. Then open Hill View Refuge.

Already have an older version? Installing this one updates it and keeps your saved marks.

iPhone or iPad?

There's no iPhone app yet. Open this website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen for a quick shortcut.
